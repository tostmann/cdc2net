Zigbee radio

Update radio firmware

Upload the radio's merged factory image (radio_h2.bin). It is staged into the radio_fw partition; the device reboots and flashes the radio if the image differs from what the radio is running (~25 s).

Stick firmware

Flash the attached stick

Writes a new firmware into the stick that is plugged into this device — over USB, no cable swap and no button. Needs a raw image starting at address 0 (avr-objcopy -O binary), not an Intel HEX file. Flash takes a second or two; the stick reboots into it by itself.

The stick's EEPROM is read before and after the write and compared, so a firmware update that would silently cost you the stick's settings shows up as EEPROM: CHANGED instead of going unnoticed. Measured on a busware CUL868 it stays untouched.

Flash does the whole sequence: it asks the stick to jump to its bootloader, waits for it to re-appear, then writes. Use Bootloader on its own only to park the stick there, e.g. to flash it from somewhere else.
