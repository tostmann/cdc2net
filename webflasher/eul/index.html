EUL — EnOcean Gateway

Transparent EnOcean (TCM515) ↔ raw-TCP bridge for the busware EUL stick.

Plug in your busware EUL, then:

The installer detects your chip (ESP32-C3 or C6) and flashes the matching build. Wi-Fi is set up right after via Improv. Use Chrome, Edge or Opera.

After flashing it appears on your network — FHEM speaks ESP3 to the TCM515 end-to-end at <ip>:2329 (like a USB300 over LAN).
