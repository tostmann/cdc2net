TUL — KNX Gateway

Transparent KNX-TP (ON-Semi NCN5130) ↔ raw-TCP bridge for the busware TUL stick.

Plug in your busware TUL, then:

The installer detects your chip (ESP32-C3 or C6) and flashes the matching build. Wi-Fi is set up right after via Improv. Use Chrome, Edge or Opera.

After flashing it appears on your network — point knxd/FHEM at <ip>:2329 (raw TP-UART, e.g. knxd -b tpuarttcp:<ip>:2329).
